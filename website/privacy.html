Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: MCS-Relay has no servers of its own. Everything the app does happens between your PC and Google (and playit.gg, if you turn it on). What it stores is on your PC and in the Google Drive folders of the servers you use. The developer sees your data only in a problem report you choose to send.

Who this policy is about

MCS-Relay is a free, open-source Windows app for hosting a Minecraft server in turns, with the world kept in a shared Google Drive folder. It is made by yeet30 (github.com/yeet30/MCS-Relay). "We" below means the people who make the app.

What the app uses from your Google account

When you sign in, you allow MCS-Relay to use:

PermissionWhat MCS-Relay does with it
Your name, email address and profile picture Shows who is signed in, and tells the other members of a server who is hosting it and who saved each version of the world.
See, edit, create and delete your Google Drive files Creates the "Minecraft Shared Servers" folder and the server folders in it; uploads and downloads the files of a server's world; reads the files other members uploaded; shares a server folder with the people you invite and removes members you remove; puts deleted servers in your Drive trash; keeps world backups you put in the cloud; and, only when you send a problem report, saves it in a "MCS-Relay problem reports" folder and shares it with the developer. MCS-Relay only works with server folders it created or that you joined, and with that reports folder. It does not open, list or change any other files in your Drive.

What is stored, and where

On your PC, in the app's data folder (%APPDATA%\mcs-relay):

  • Your Google sign-in tokens, encrypted with Windows' own data protection.
  • Your settings, the folder each server uses on your PC, and what the app knows about each server's files (names, sizes and checksums), so it only transfers what changed.
  • A record of an upload that was interrupted, so it can be finished later.
  • Copies of your own files, only when you choose to keep the cloud's version over yours.
  • If you link playit.gg: the key of this PC's playit.gg agent, encrypted with Windows' own data protection, your playit.gg address, and playit.gg's agent program (playit in the same folder).
  • Java the app downloaded for a server, if you let it (java in the same folder).
  • A log of what the app did (logs in the same folder, at most about 8 MB): server names, folder paths, the names and email addresses of members, and errors. It never holds your password or your Google sign-in. It leaves your PC only inside a problem report you choose to send.

In each server folder on your PC: the world backups you make, in mcs-relay-backups. They are never synced.

In Google Drive, inside each server folder (stored by Google in the Drive of whoever owns each file):

  • The server's files: the world, its settings and anything else in the server folder that is not on its ignore list.
  • A small .launcher folder with the app's own files: who is hosting right now (name and email), the players online, the server's playit.gg addresses, the list of the world's files, and the name and email of the member who saved each version.
  • The world backups members put in the cloud, in .launcher/backups. They count against the Drive storage of whoever uploaded them.

In a problem report, only when you send one: what you wrote, a few lines about the app and your PC (versions, Windows version, what the app was doing) and, if you leave it ticked, the end of the log above. It is saved as a text file in a "MCS-Relay problem reports" folder in your Drive and shared, read only, with the developer, who gets an email about it with your name and email address. Delete the file to take it back. You can also save a report as a file instead and send it however you like.

Apart from a problem report you send, we have no copy of any of this. There is no MCS-Relay account and no MCS-Relay server.

Who can see it

  • The members of a server you are in, through Google Drive sharing: the server's files and its cloud backups, and your name and email address as a member and as the host or author of a version.
  • The developer, only for a problem report you send, as described above. It is used only to find and fix the problem.
  • Google, which provides Google Drive and Google sign-in, under Google's privacy policy.
  • playit.gg, only if you use it, under playit.gg's privacy policy. When you link it, you sign in to playit.gg (or create an account) on playit.gg's own website: MCS-Relay never sees your playit.gg password, and your Google account is not shared with playit.gg. MCS-Relay then uses playit.gg's service with this PC's agent key to make your Minecraft tunnel and read its address, and runs playit.gg's agent while you host, so players reach your server through playit.gg, which sees their connections. If you chose a playit.gg program yourself instead, MCS-Relay only starts it.
  • GitHub, where the app checks for new versions and downloads them, and from where it downloads playit.gg's agent and Java when you use those. Like any download, this shows GitHub your IP address.
  • Mojang, PaperMC, FabricMC and Adoptium, only when you download a server program or Java in the app: it fetches the list of versions and the download from their official services, which, like any download, see your IP address. Java is Eclipse Temurin from the Adoptium project.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or collect statistics about how you use the app. The app contains no analytics or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MCS-Relay's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to provide the features described above, is never transferred except as those features need (sharing a server with its members, and a problem report you choose to send to the developer), and is never used for advertising.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in the app's menu: your sign-in is removed from this PC.
  • Take the app's access back in your Google Account under third-party apps and services.
  • Delete a server you own in Settings > Servers: its folder goes to your Drive trash, and Google empties the trash after 30 days. Leave a server you joined to remove your access to it.
  • Delete a backup in the app's Backups window, and the mcs-relay-backups folder in a server folder to remove them all from this PC.
  • Unlink playit.gg in Settings > Scripts: its key is removed from this PC and its tunnel deleted. The agent stays in your playit.gg account until you delete it there.
  • Delete a problem report from the "MCS-Relay problem reports" folder in your Drive: the developer loses access to it.
  • Uninstall MCS-Relay and delete the data folder above to remove everything it kept on your PC.

Security

Sign-in tokens and the playit.gg key are encrypted on your PC, and every connection to Google and playit.gg uses HTTPS. Everything the app downloads (server programs, playit.gg's agent, Java) is checked against the checksum its official source publishes before it is used. The app's source code is public, so anyone can check what it does.

Children

MCS-Relay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Children's Google accounts follow the rules their parents set in Google Family Link.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Every earlier version stays visible in the project's history on GitHub.
