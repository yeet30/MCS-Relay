Play only with people you trust

This is the most important part of these terms.

  • A server in MCS-Relay is a Google Drive folder shared between its members. Every member who can play can upload changes to any file in it.
  • When you host, MCS-Relay starts the Minecraft server from that folder on your PC: Java runs the server .jar with its mods, plugins and libraries. These files are programs. They run with the same rights as your Windows account, so they can read, change and send your files like any program you open yourself.
  • A member with bad intentions, or a member whose Google account or PC was taken over, could therefore put harmful software into the server folder, and it would run on the PC of the next member who hosts.
  • MCS-Relay does not check, scan or isolate the files members upload, and it cannot tell a harmful mod from a good one.

So:

  • Only create, join and invite people to servers with people you trust. Give "Can play" only to people you trust; "Can view" members cannot change files.
  • Keep your antivirus on and Windows up to date.
  • If mods, plugins or programs appear in a server that nobody told you about, ask the other members before you host it.
  • Remove members you no longer trust. They keep whatever they already downloaded.

Your data

What the app does with your Google account and Drive is described in the privacy policy. In short, MCS-Relay has no servers of its own: it works between your PC and Google.

Your worlds and backups

A server's files are kept in Google Drive and take up the storage of the members who upload them. MCS-Relay tries hard not to lose anything, but bugs, conflicts, other members and the services it relies on can still damage or overwrite a world. Keep your own backup of any world you care about.
