## MCS-Relay 1.2.1

### New
- **Hosting history**: the tab on the left edge of the window slides out who hosted the server, when, for how long and who played, with the session running now on top. Sessions are recorded from this version on, by everyone who has it.

### Changes
- **The modpack** is now a small button in the server picker instead of a whole line, so the address and the progress stay where they were. Click it for the pack's version and its Modrinth page.
- **Online players** got a new look: who is online with a coloured initial, the player count and the uptime.

### Fixes
- **Clicking a notification** (playit.gg linked, a start or stop finished) brings MCS-Relay to the front, even when it was minimized or behind another window.
- **The console button** takes you all the way to the console while the server is printing, and shows its newest line.
